Passenger Terms & Conditions

Please read the following Terms and Conditions governing all bookings, transfers, and private chauffeur journeys booked with J Transport Group Ltd (TfL Licence 01103601).

1. Booking Cancellation & Refund

  • Cancellations made 24 hours or more before the scheduled pickup time are eligible for a 100% refund.
  • Cancellations made between 12 and 24 hours before the scheduled pickup time are eligible for a 60% refund.
  • Cancellations made less than 12 hours before the scheduled pickup time are non-refundable.

2. Child Seats

Child seats are available on request and are subject to availability. A charge of £10 per child seat applies. Customers should request child seats at the time of booking.

3. Waiting Time

For airport pickups, the driver will monitor the flight where flight details have been provided. Any included waiting period will be as specified in the booking confirmation. Additional waiting time may incur extra charges.

4. Passenger No-Show

If the passenger does not arrive at the agreed pickup location within the applicable waiting period and cannot be contacted, the booking may be treated as a no-show and the fare will be non-refundable.

5. Pickup & Drop-off Information

Passengers are responsible for providing accurate pickup, destination, flight and contact details at the time of booking. J Transport Group Ltd cannot be held responsible for delays or additional charges resulting from incorrect or incomplete information.

6. Luggage

Passengers must ensure that the amount and size of luggage are suitable for the vehicle booked. If the luggage exceeds the vehicle's available capacity, J Transport Group Ltd may need to arrange a larger vehicle, subject to availability and additional charges.

7. Passenger Behaviour & Vehicle Damage

Passengers must behave respectfully towards the driver and other passengers and must not cause damage to the vehicle. Any costs arising from deliberate or negligent damage, excessive cleaning, or contamination of the vehicle may be charged to the passenger.

8. Journey Delays

J Transport Group Ltd is not responsible for delays caused by circumstances beyond its reasonable control, including traffic congestion, road closures, accidents, severe weather, strikes, or other unforeseen events. We will make reasonable efforts to minimise the impact of such delays.

9. Changes to Booking

Any changes to the pickup time, pickup location, destination, passenger numbers or vehicle requirements should be requested as soon as possible. Changes are subject to availability and may result in additional charges.

10. Driver & Vehicle Safety

Passengers must follow reasonable instructions given by the driver for safety purposes. Seat belts must be worn where provided, and passengers must not request the driver to undertake any unsafe or unlawful action. The driver may refuse a journey where passenger behaviour or circumstances present a safety risk.

J Transport Group Ltd provides 24/7 first-class airport transfer and chauffeur services across London and surrounding UK side areas.
