Child Seat Guide for Family Travel

When travelling with children, let J Transport Group LTD know your child’s age and seat requirements during booking. This guide explains our available toddler and booster seat options.

Request a Child Seat
01 · TODDLER SEAT

Toddler Seat

A forward-facing seat for toddlers. Share your child’s age, height, and weight so we can provide the suitable seat.

Weight
9–18 kg / 20–40 lbs
Age
Approx. 15 months–4 years
Height
76–105 cm
Group
Group 1

What to share when requesting

  • Child’s current age and approximate height.
  • Approximate weight if known.
  • Outbound, return, or both journeys.
02 · BOOSTER SEAT

Booster Seat

A booster elevates your child to ensure the seat belt fits comfortably and securely.

Weight
15–36 kg / 33–80 lbs
Age
Approx. 3.5–12 years
Height
100–135 cm
Group
Groups 2/3

Before your journey

  • Request your booster seat when making your booking.
  • Provide your child's age & height.
